# Shifted Hales–Jewett word on an actual coordinate ray

**4 November 2026 — draft, full CI required.**

The verified interleaved singleton schedule (#507), genuine fixed-root
D₂ word (#508), and exact typed ray-slot decoder (#509) provide the
right geometry and bounded addresses. This module provides the missing
ACTUAL D₀ variable-word lift for any one scheduled coordinate.

A proper finite Hales–Jewett line w of length N with first variable
index r is translated into the coordinate's N ray slots. The true
variable is at slotPos(i,r); all variable occurrences are descendants
because all variable positions are ≥r. Outside the coordinate's slots
the word is fixed to the fallback letter. The singleton support is
checked as a genuine 1-complete-skew tree, and the evaluated span is
exactly the embedded Hales–Jewett line, including its converse.

The full interleaved Ramsey theorem still needs the **vector** word
on an arbitrary no-D₁ kind function, a mixed Element parameter
representation and equality of observed q-marker letters. The D₂
word must be located at the PREDETERMINED root offset(i), not at a
root depending on the Hales–Jewett variable index r. No claim about
higher k, true D₁ leaf sets, relative refinement or Lemma 27 is made.
