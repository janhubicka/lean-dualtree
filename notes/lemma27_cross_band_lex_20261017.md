# Equal final-rank lex ordering: two projected cone comparison lemmas

The sorted forward cut frontier order is length-first ForwardAux,
not ordinary lex. The repaired Lemma 27 final skew clause II
requires comparing projected ambient lengths conditioned on
ordinary forward lex at the SAME final intrinsic rank.

For equal T frontier ranks, original complete T skew clause II
forces length-first frontier order to agree with ordinary lex
order, and incomparability of distinct frontier cones transfers
this order to EVERY pair of projected descendants.

For frontier bands m versus m+1, the higher-rank frontier
has a true T predecessor s of intrinsic rank m. As the
higher frontier is minimal outside the cut, s is retained.
The checked rank-m retained/frontier geometry makes
s lex before and prefix-incomparable with every rank-m
outside-cut frontier. Projected descendants of the
rank-(m+1) frontier stay lex before all projected descendants
of the rank-m frontiers.

This supplies the previously missing lex orientation for
cross-frontier final skew clause II. It does not yet prove
the full equal-rank length inequality; this must combine
the orientation with exact T projected rank formulas,
vector local rank/length inequalities and original T skew
clauses II/III. For same-frontier pairs, local lex order
reflection is also needed.

No global final skew or published reversed-starred
domain equivalence is assumed.
