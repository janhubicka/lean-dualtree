# Two-rank local separation: adversarial proof obligation

The original local vector-complete axiom separates intrinsic level r from
r+1 across *all* ordered coordinate pairs. A known root w of local rank
s>=r+2 has an actual support predecessor u of rank r+1, by truncating
the canonical inverse address of w in its corrected forward-complete
support. Thus |z|<|u|<|w| for every local root z of rank r in every
coordinate. Over the naturals, |z|+2<=|w|.

This requires neither a new vector-skew hypothesis nor an illegal
change in the mixed-product domain. The important point is that
|P_i(z)| is NOT the local rank, and neither is height_T(P_i(z)):
the actual projected T height is H_i + |z|.

This closes the *numeric* reverse frontier band gap:
if H_i=m+1, H_j=m, H_i+r<H_j+s, then r+2<=s,
therefore |z|+2<=|w| and
H_i+|z| < H_j+|w|. The final ambient length conclusion
then follows from skew clause III of the **original** T.

An additional Lean theorem for projected/projection pairs and
ordinary lex ordering at equal final ranks is still necessary;
final complete skew and the global f' remain open.
