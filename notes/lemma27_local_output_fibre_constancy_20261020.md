# Fibrewise constraints imposed on target output O

To make the pointwise finite Q-auxiliary letters into a genuine
mixed-product Ramsey-span point, the assignments must be constant
on each local component variable fibre, and genuine local
constants must remain their original letters.

The new proofs exploit two already formalized source facts:

* At a true shortened projection P_i(z), the final interpolated
  ambient word f' reads precisely the original component word
  symbol f_i(z), translated to the selected ambient root type.
* Over a two-letter alphabet, O.span ⊆ f'.span gives a uniform
  syntactic substitution of f' into O.

Consequently O's **actual dependent word symbols**, not just
its evaluated words, agree at P_i(z) and P_i(v) whenever
f_i(z)=v. Original local constants are preserved at P_i(z).
No unverified rank-filtered root correspondence is postulated.

The next step is now to choose enlarged Q input letters once
per local variable v, encoding O at P_i(v). This will be a
literal member of the enlarged mixed-product span via the
verified alphabet-lifting construction. The exact Q decoder
is independent of the local evaluation position inside a
fixed frontier, so it reproduces O on every local fibre.

Still separate: existence of appropriate D1/D2 input markers
and full starred-tree equality, including the corrected
ordering requirement.
