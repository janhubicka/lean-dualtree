# Terminal safety from actual final-support inclusion

The generic necessary condition from PR #408 says every terminal
of a real corrected Q output has a safe projection address. The
word-refinement hypothesis by itself does not imply that of an
arbitrary starred target O.

The relevant geometric hypothesis is instead:
`O.tree ⊆ finalBudgetSupport g.core f (m+q)`, with the ACTUAL
rank-filtered support T'_M, not the unfiltered projected envelope.

The new proof has two independent steps:

1. Every noninterior terminal of a forward semi-complete starred
   word in positive arity is strictly after its maximal interior
   cut. This follows from the verified fullBefore property.
2. Every vertex of T'_M after the inclusive cut comes from one
   selected projected local root and therefore has safeAddress.

Equal actual complete corrected signatures identify O's maximal
interior cut with the original source cut. Combining 1 and 2
proves that all O terminals have safe local addresses.

This DISCHARGES the terminal-safety issue conditionally on
explicit tree-support inclusion; it does not show that word-only
span inclusion supplies that condition. It also does not choose
the particular D2 bullet markers, prove their membership in
local variable supports, or satisfy the required corrected
starred vector-one order for those exact points.

Next focus: identify the source literal R markers with the target
terminals via the equal signature trees; transport each leaf's
unique safe (i,z) to the corresponding D2 index; then audit
whether the resulting local points belong to f's D2 supports
and are correctly ordered. Full starred Q-object equality
would follow only after those checks.
