**6 November 2026.** Depends on merged #512 and #513.

Goal: For arbitrary genuinely interleaved D0 and D2 coordinates, with
NoUp and intrinsic k=1, prove every element of the true
MixedProduct.VariableWord.span is parameterized by independent D0
Hales–Jewett variables and independent observed D2 letters.

KVariableWord and the fixed-root D2 KVariableWord, reconstructing
the complete mixed Element (words, empty upPoint function, fixed D2

not assume Good, a pre-existing homogeneous word, or a uniform colour.

the genuine span is nonempty; positive c-good existence for every
finite colouring; uniqueness of colours factoring through the D2
record. Those remain independent proof gates.

Require complete `lake build DualTree`, finite Q regressions, source
minimal-import audit, and the updated distinct transitive axiom audit.
